Privacy

Mailing list

If you subscribe, your email address is sent to EmailOctopus, which manages our mailing list and sends our confirmation emails and updates. The form uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent automated signups. You can unsubscribe using the link in our mailing-list emails.

Website services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It loads some fonts from Google Fonts and the signup form from EmailOctopus. These services receive connection information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests their content.

Contact

For questions about the mailing list or your information, email hello@pandorasboxchoir.org. Our email is handled through Google Workspace.
